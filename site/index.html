Features

Built for people who draw control panels every day.

Schematics with automatic cross-references

A3 pages with frame and title block, an IEC 60617 symbol library and wires that behave like conductors.

  • Coil and contacts linked by tag, with contact image
  • Interruption points that pair by name, star sources
  • Wire numbers by pattern: {pagina}.{n:2}, per cabinet, reserved ranges
  • Schematics in two languages at once from a dictionary

Terminal strips, cables and connectors

Terminals are read from the schematic, sorted and documented without retyping anything.

  • Multi-level terminals, jumpers, spares and accessories
  • Cables with conductors, colours and shields; cable lists
  • Automatic terminal strip and cable diagram pages

PLC and industrial networks

Cards, stations and I/O points on the schematic, with addresses in the format of your PLC.

  • Siemens TIA I0.0 · IEC 61131 %IX0.0 · TwinCAT AT %I*
  • Tag tables for TIA Portal, GVL and PLCopen XML for CODESYS and TwinCAT
  • Profinet, EtherCAT and Modbus diagrams; IP and Profinet name table

Cabinet layout and wiring

Place the devices of the schematic on the mounting plate and get the numbers the workshop needs.

  • DIN rails and cable ducts with fill level
  • Automatic placement, drilling plan, estimated wire lengths
  • Wiring sequence and marking labels for common printers

An AI assistant that edits the project

Ask in plain words: add a motor starter, renumber a page, explain a check. Use your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini key; nothing leaves your computer otherwise.

  • Every change can be undone like any other edit
  • A plain-text project format designed for AI agents
  • Command line for batch exports and checks

And much more

Everything a real project needs.

Lists and reports

Bill of materials, purchase list, devices, wires, terminals, cables and PLC lists as pages or Excel.

Project checks

Open contacts, duplicate tags, unconnected pins, missing parts: found before the workshop does.

Manufacturer parts

Import part data from EDZ packages, XML and CSV into a local SQLite library.

Revisions

Archive a revision, compare two versions of a project and mark the changed pages.

Macros and title blocks

Reusable circuits, a component editor, custom title blocks and page templates.

Six languages

Interface and manual in English, Italian, French, German, Spanish and Russian.
